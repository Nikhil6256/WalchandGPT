WALCHAND COLLEGE OF ENGINEERING, SANGLI
CURRENT NOTICES, ADMISSION PROCEDURES & CAMPUS EVENTS (AY 2026-27)

1. CENTRALIZED ADMISSION PROCESS (CAP) REPORTING (AY 2026-27)
- Centralized Admission Process for B.Tech First Year and Direct Second Year (DSE):
  * Governed by State CET Cell, Maharashtra State.
  * Reporting guidelines active for CAP Round 1, CAP Round 2, CAP Round 3, and CAP Round 4.
  * All candidates allotted seats must report with original documents (SSC/HSC marksheets, MHT-CET/JEE scorecard, Domicile/Nationality, Caste/Validity/NCL if applicable) and admission fees at the WCE Admission Reporting Center.

2. NEW PROGRAM ADMISSIONS:
- Master of Business Administration (MBA):
  * Option forms can be filled through candidate login on the State CET Cell Portal starting from CAP Round-2.
  * Approved by AICTE, affiliated with Shivaji University.
  * Specialized in Operations, Finance, Tech Management, and Marketing.

- Bachelor of Design (B.Des):
  * First-of-its-kind engineering-adjacent design degree at WCE.
  * Admission based on design aptitude and 10+2 merit via State CET Cell.

- Integrated B.Tech - M.Tech Programme (5 Years):
  * Specialization in VLSI Design & Embedded Systems.
  * Directly addresses the critical skilled talent requirement in the Indian and global semiconductor manufacturing sector.
