WALCHAND COLLEGE OF ENGINEERING, SANGLI
DEPARTMENTS, ACADEMIC PROGRAMS & INTAKE CAPACITY

1. OVERVIEW OF DEPARTMENTS
Walchand College of Engineering houses the following academic departments:
- Department of Computer Science and Engineering (CSE)
- Department of Information Technology (IT)
- Department of Artificial Intelligence and Machine Learning (AI & ML)
- Department of Robotics and Automation
- Department of Electronics Engineering
- Department of Electrical Engineering
- Department of Mechanical Engineering
- Department of Civil Engineering
- Department of Applied Mechanics
- Department of Design
- Department of Humanities and Sciences
- Centre of Inter & Multidisciplinary Research Collaboration and Learning Excellence (CIRCLE)
- Polytechnic (Diploma) Wing

2. UNDERGRADUATE (B.TECH) PROGRAMS (4 YEARS)
- B.Tech in Computer Science and Engineering:
  * Established: 1984
  * Intake: 120 (CAP) + Supernumerary quotas (EWS, TFWS, NRI/PIO)
  * Accreditation: NBA Accredited
  * Focus areas: Algorithms, Cloud Computing, Operating Systems, Machine Learning, Cyber Security, Full-Stack Software Engineering.
  * Labs: High Performance Computing Lab, Advanced Networking Lab, Database Systems Lab, Software Engineering Lab.

- B.Tech in Information Technology:
  * Established: 2001
  * Intake: 60 (CAP) + TFWS/EWS
  * Accreditation: NBA Accredited
  * Focus areas: Web & Mobile App Development, Distributed Systems, Cloud Architecture, DevOps, Data Analytics.

- B.Tech in Artificial Intelligence and Machine Learning (AI & ML):
  * Newly expanded cutting-edge specialization
  * Focus areas: Deep Learning, Natural Language Processing, Computer Vision, Generative AI, Reinforcement Learning, MLOps.

- B.Tech in Robotics and Automation:
  * Multidisciplinary branch blending mechanical, electrical, and computer intelligence.
  * Focus areas: Industrial Robotics, Kinematics, Autonomous Systems, ROS (Robot Operating System), Embedded Controllers, Machine Vision.

- B.Tech in Electronics Engineering:
  * Established: 1986
  * Intake: 60
  * Accreditation: NBA Accredited
  * Focus areas: Embedded Systems, VLSI Design, DSP, Communication Engineering, IoT Architecture.

- B.Tech in Electrical Engineering:
  * Established: 1955
  * Intake: 60
  * Accreditation: NBA Accredited
  * Focus areas: Power Systems, Smart Grids, Electric Vehicle (EV) Technology, Renewable Energy, Industrial Automation & Drives (Siemens Lab).

- B.Tech in Mechanical Engineering:
  * Established: 1955
  * Intake: 60
  * Accreditation: NBA Accredited
  * Focus areas: Thermal Engineering, Design & Finite Element Analysis, CAD/CAM/CAE (Dassault Lab), Production and Mechatronics.

- B.Tech in Civil Engineering:
  * Established: 1947 (Founding department)
  * Intake: 60
  * Accreditation: NBA Accredited
  * Focus areas: Structural Engineering, Geotechnical Engineering, Environmental Engineering, Transportation, GIS & Remote Sensing.

3. NEW SPECIALIZED UNDERGRADUATE & DUAL-DEGREE PROGRAMS
- Bachelor of Design (B.Des):
  * Focus on Product Design, Industrial Design, User Experience (UX/UI), and Sustainable Design.
  * Supported by the AICTE IDEA Lab and rapid prototyping equipment.

- Integrated B.Tech - M.Tech Programme (5 Years):
  * Specialization: VLSI Design and Embedded Systems
  * Integrated 5-year curriculum preparing students directly for leading semiconductor firms (NVIDIA, Intel, Qualcomm, Texas Instruments, AMD).

4. POSTGRADUATE (M.TECH & MBA) PROGRAMS
- Master of Technology (M.Tech) (2 Years):
  * M.Tech in Civil Engineering (Structural Engineering)
  * M.Tech in Civil Engineering (Environmental Engineering)
  * M.Tech in Mechanical Engineering (Design Engineering)
  * M.Tech in Mechanical Engineering (Production Engineering)
  * M.Tech in Electrical Engineering (Power Systems)
  * M.Tech in Electrical Engineering (Control Systems)
  * M.Tech in Electronics Engineering
  * M.Tech in Computer Science and Engineering

- Master of Business Administration (MBA) (2 Years):
  * Newly introduced program under Shivaji University.
  * Specializations in Operations & Supply Chain, Technology Management, Finance, Human Resources, and Marketing.
  * Admission through Maharashtra MBA-CET / CAT / CMAT through Centralized Admission Process (CAP).

5. POLYTECHNIC (DIPLOMA) PROGRAMS (3 YEARS)
- Diploma in Civil Engineering
- Diploma in Mechanical Engineering
- Diploma in Electrical Engineering
- Diploma in Industrial Electronics Engineering

6. DOCTORAL RESEARCH (PH.D.)
- Recognized research center under Shivaji University Kolhapur.
- Doctoral scholars conduct research under QIP (Quality Improvement Programme), TEQIP sponsorships, and industry-sponsored grants.
