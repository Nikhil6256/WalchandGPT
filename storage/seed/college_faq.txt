WALCHAND COLLEGE OF ENGINEERING — FREQUENTLY ASKED QUESTIONS

=== GENERAL INFORMATION ===

Q: What is Walchand College of Engineering?
A: Walchand College of Engineering (WCE), Sangli, is a premier autonomous engineering institution established in 1947. It is affiliated with Savitribai Phule Pune University (SPPU) and is approved by AICTE. The college is NAAC Grade A+ accredited and NBA accredited for multiple programs.

Q: Where is WCE located?
A: Walchand College of Engineering is located in Vishrambag, Sangli, Maharashtra - 416415, India.

Q: What are the departments at WCE?
A: WCE offers programs in:
- Computer Engineering
- Information Technology
- Electronics & Telecommunication Engineering
- Mechanical Engineering
- Civil Engineering
- Chemical Engineering
- Instrumentation Engineering

Q: What programs does WCE offer?
A: WCE offers:
- Bachelor of Engineering (B.E.) — 4-year undergraduate programs
- Master of Engineering (M.E.) — 2-year postgraduate programs
- Ph.D. programs in various disciplines
- MBA program

=== ADMISSION ===

Q: How do I get admission to WCE?
A: Admission to B.E. programs is through Maharashtra CET (MHT-CET) and JEE Main scores via Centralized Admission Process (CAP) conducted by the State CET Cell. For M.E., admission is through GATE scores.

Q: What is the intake capacity?
A: Approximate intake per branch per year:
- Computer Engineering: 120 students
- Information Technology: 60 students
- E&TC Engineering: 60 students
- Mechanical Engineering: 120 students
- Civil Engineering: 60 students
- Chemical Engineering: 60 students

=== ACADEMIC POLICIES ===

Q: What is the minimum attendance requirement?
A: Students must maintain a minimum of 75% attendance in each subject to be eligible to appear for the semester examinations. Students with attendance between 65-75% may apply for attendance condonation with valid medical/other reasons.

Q: What is the grading system?
A: WCE follows the Savitribai Phule Pune University grading system:
- O (Outstanding): 91-100
- A+ (Excellent): 81-90
- A (Very Good): 71-80
- B+ (Good): 61-70
- B (Above Average): 51-60
- C (Average): 41-50
- P (Pass): 33-40
- F (Fail): Below 33

Q: How many credits are required to complete B.E.?
A: A student must earn a minimum of 240 credits over 8 semesters to be awarded the B.E. degree.

Q: What is the CGPA calculation method?
A: CGPA = Sum of (Grade Points × Credits) / Total Credits earned

=== EXAMINATIONS ===

Q: How are end-semester examinations conducted?
A: End-semester examinations are conducted by SPPU for most subjects. Internal assessments (ISE) are conducted by the college. The distribution is typically:
- End Semester Examination: 70 marks
- Internal Semester Examination: 30 marks (Unit Tests + assignments)

Q: What happens if I fail a subject?
A: Students who fail can appear for:
1. KT (Keep Term) examinations — appearing with the next regular batch
2. ATKT (Allowed To Keep Term) — can continue to next semester with backlog subjects

Q: Can I get my answer sheet re-evaluated?
A: Yes, students can apply for revaluation/reassessment of answer sheets within 15 days of result declaration by paying the prescribed fee.

Q: What are the library timings?
A: The central library is open:
- Monday to Saturday: 8:00 AM to 10:00 PM
- Sunday: 10:00 AM to 6:00 PM
- During examination period: Extended hours until midnight
