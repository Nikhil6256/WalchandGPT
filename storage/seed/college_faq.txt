=== 1. GENERAL INFORMATION & HISTORY ===

Q: What is Walchand College of Engineering?
A: Walchand College of Engineering (WCE), Sangli, is a prestigious government-aided autonomous engineering institution established in 1947. Affiliated with Shivaji University, Kolhapur, it is one of the oldest and most reputed engineering colleges in India, celebrated for over 75 years of academic excellence (Platinum Jubilee celebrated in 2021). WCE holds NAAC accreditation and NBA accreditation for its core engineering programs.

Q: What is the history and legacy of WCE?
A: 
- 1947: Founded by the Late Shri Dhondumama Sathe with a single program in Civil Engineering (B.E. Civil).
- 1955: Renamed in honor of Seth Walchand Hirachand as Walchand College of Engineering; launched Mechanical and Electrical branches along with Diploma programs.
- 1956-1965: Administrative Council established with industry participation (Walchand Industries, HCC, IHP).
- 1971: Introduced Postgraduate programs and established the first recognized research center under Shivaji University.
- 1977: First private engineering college in Maharashtra to receive government aid.
- 2003: First college under Shivaji University to secure NBA Accreditation for five UG programs.
- 2005: Selected for World Bank-assisted TEQIP scheme for institutional transformation.
- 2007: Awarded Autonomous Institute status.
- 2008: Inauguration of the 50,000 sq. ft. Ajit Gulabchand Central Library.
- 2021: Celebrated Platinum Jubilee (75 years of excellence).

Q: Which university is WCE affiliated with?
A: Walchand College of Engineering is an autonomous institute affiliated with Shivaji University, Kolhapur. It designs its own academic curriculum, syllabi, and examination system under autonomous regulations while degrees are conferred by Shivaji University.

=== 3. ADMISSION PROCEDURES & ELIGIBILITY ===

Q: How do I get admission to B.Tech at WCE?
A: Admission to First Year B.Tech is administered through the Centralized Admission Process (CAP) conducted by the Maharashtra State Common Entrance Test Cell (CET Cell).
- Eligibility: Passed 10+2 / HSC with Physics and Mathematics as compulsory subjects along with Chemistry/Biotechnology/Biology/Technical Vocational subjects.
- Entrance Exams: Valid score in MHT-CET (for Maharashtra State candidates) or JEE (Main) Paper-1 (for All India candidates).
- Quotas: CAP General Quota, Direct Second Year (DSE) for diploma holders, and NRI / PIO / OCI / FN / CIWGC categories.

Q: How can students apply for MBA at WCE?
A: Admission to the MBA program is through the Maharashtra MBA/MMS CET or equivalent national exams (CAT, CMAT, MAT) via the State CET Cell CAP rounds. Eligible students submit college options through the CET Cell portal.

Q: How do I get admission to M.Tech at WCE?
A: Admission to M.Tech programs requires a relevant B.E./B.Tech degree with a qualifying GATE score through the State CET Cell CAP process or sponsored/institute-level rounds.

=== 8. ACADEMIC RULES, ATTENDANCE & EXAMINATION POLICIES ===

Q: What is the attendance policy and minimum attendance required at WCE?
A: Walchand College of Engineering strictly enforces a minimum attendance of 75% in each registered theory course, tutorial, practical/laboratory session, workshop, and seminar per semester, in accordance with WCE Autonomous Academic Regulations and Shivaji University / AICTE norms.
- Condonation: Up to 10% relaxation (allowing a minimum 65% attendance) may be granted by the Director / Dean Academics strictly on valid medical grounds (with doctor's certificate submitted within 7 days) or officially sanctioned institute-level representation in sports, technical competitions, hackathons, or NCC/NSS.
- Detention ('XX' Grade): Students failing to secure 75% attendance (or 65% with approved condonation) are awarded an 'XX' grade and detained. Detained students are not permitted to appear for the End-Semester Examination (ESE) and must re-register for the course.
- Monitoring: Attendance is updated weekly on the College MIS / ERP portal. Monthly defaulter lists are displayed on department notice boards.

Q: What is the examination and evaluation system at WCE?
A: WCE follows a continuous 100-mark evaluation structure:
- In-Semester Evaluation (ISE): 20% weightage (tests, quizzes, assignments).
- Mid-Semester Examination (MSE): 30% weightage (Units 1-3).
- End-Semester Examination (ESE): 50% weightage (comprehensive syllabus).
Passing requires at least 40% in ESE (20/50 marks) and an aggregate of 40% across ISE + MSE + ESE.
