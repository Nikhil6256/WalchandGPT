6. CAREER DEVELOPMENT CELL (CDC) & PLACEMENT PROCESS
═══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════

The Career Development Cell (CDC) at WCE coordinates all placement activities:

6A. PRE-PLACEMENT TRAINING (PPT):
  - Technical: Data Structures & Algorithms, System Design, Database (SQL/NoSQL),
    Operating Systems, Computer Networks, OOPS, Coding contests.
  - Aptitude: Quantitative, Logical Reasoning, Verbal Ability.
  - Soft Skills: Communication, Group Discussions, Mock Interviews, Resume Building.
  - Platform: Students are encouraged to practise on LeetCode, HackerRank,
    Codeforces, and CodeChef.

6B. INTERNSHIP & INDUSTRIAL TRAINING:
  - Mandatory 6-month industrial internship for final-year students (all branches).
  - Summer internship (6–8 weeks) for pre-final year students.
  - Internship partners include: Siemens, Cummins, John Deere, Persistent Systems,
    KPIT Technologies, Rubiscape, Forbes Marshall, ISRO, DRDO, Thermax, L&T.
  - Many students receive Pre-Placement Offers (PPOs) from their internship companies.

6C. PLACEMENT SCHEDULE & PROCESS:
  - Phase 1 (August–October): Dream & Super Dream companies (CTC > ₹15 LPA).
  - Phase 2 (November–January): Core IT and mass recruiters.
  - Phase 3 (February–April): Core engineering companies, PSUs, and Open drives.
  - Process: Online Test / Coding Round → Technical Interviews (1–3 rounds) → HR Round → Offer Letter.

6D. INDUSTRY-SPONSORED PROJECTS:
  - Final Year capstone projects sponsored and guided jointly by industry partners
    (Siemens, Dassault, TCS, KPIT, Persistent, Rubiscape, etc.) and WCE faculty.
  - Encourages innovation and real-world problem-solving.

9. FREQUENTLY ASKED PLACEMENT QUESTIONS
═══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════

Q: Which companies come to WCE for campus recruitment?
A: 300+ companies visit WCE every year. Top ones include Google, Microsoft, Amazon,
   Rubrik, CRED, Qualcomm, Texas Instruments, Oracle, Dassault Systèmes, Veritas,
   TCS, Infosys, Wipro, LTI Mindtree, Capgemini, Cognizant, Accenture,
   Tech Mahindra, Siemens, Tata Motors, John Deere, Bharat Forge, Cummins, L&T,
   Bosch, Mahindra, ISRO, and DRDO (see detailed list in Section 3 above).

Q: What is the highest package at WCE?
A: The highest package ever recorded at WCE is ₹1.50 Crore per Annum
   (in AY 2024-25) for an international/Tier-1 software role. The previous
   milestone was ₹1.30 Crore/Annum by Saurabh Pukale (2021 batch).

Q: What is the average package at WCE?
A: The overall average CTC is ₹12 LPA. For CSE and IT branches, the average
   is higher, ranging from ₹18 to ₹22 LPA.

Q: What branches get the best placements?
A: CSE, IT, AI-ML, and Electronics consistently see the highest packages
   and placement rates. CSE has the highest average of ~₹22 LPA.

Q: Do companies offer internships through WCE?
A: Yes. Siemens, Cummins, John Deere, Persistent Systems, KPIT Technologies,
   Rubiscape, Forbes Marshall, ISRO, DRDO, and Thermax are regular internship
   partners. Many internships convert to Pre-Placement Offers (PPOs).

Q: Does WCE place students in PSUs (Government companies)?
A: Yes. ISRO, DRDO, NTPC, BPCL, GAIL, MSEDCL, and ONGC recruit from WCE.
   Students with GATE scores are also eligible for PSU recruitment.

Q: What is the placement process / selection procedure at WCE?
A: Typically: Online Aptitude/Coding Test → Technical Interview Round 1 →
   Technical Interview Round 2 (for some companies) → HR Interview → Offer Letter.
   Top companies (Google, Microsoft, Amazon) may have 3–5 interview rounds.

Q: Who to contact for placement-related queries?
A: The Career Development Cell (CDC) at WCE Sangli.
   Website: https://walchandsangli.ac.in/student-careers/career-development-cell/
   The CDC is managed by dedicated faculty coordinators from each department.

Q: Does WCE have 100% placement?
A: WCE provides 100% placement assistance and career guidance to all
   registered students. Approximately 95% of eligible students receive at
   least one job offer during the campus placement season.

Q: What is the median salary package at WCE?
A: The median CTC across all branches is approximately ₹8.5 LPA.

Q: Which IT companies visit WCE?
A: Major IT companies visiting WCE include: TCS, Infosys, Wipro, Accenture,
   Capgemini, Cognizant, Tech Mahindra, LTI Mindtree, Persistent Systems,
   Hexaware, Zensar, Mphasis, KPIT Technologies, Epam, GlobalLogic, Nagarro,
   and Infobeans.

Q: Which product-based companies visit WCE?
A: Top product companies: Google, Microsoft, Amazon, Rubrik, CRED, Oracle,
   Dassault Systèmes, Veritas, Qualcomm, Texas Instruments, STMicroelectronics,
   NXP, Microchip, Synopsys, Cadence Design, ARM, Fractal Analytics, CleverTap,
   Rubiscape, GlobalLogic, and Epam Systems.

Q: Do mechanical or civil students get placed?
A: Yes. Core companies like Siemens, Tata Motors, John Deere, Bharat Forge,
   Cummins, L&T, Thermax, Bosch, Kirloskar, Atlas Copco, Eaton, BPCL, NTPC,
   and DRDO recruit Mechanical and Civil students. Average CTC for Mechanical
   is ~₹8 LPA and Civil is ~₹7 LPA.

Q: Is there an on-campus placement for MBA students at WCE?
A: The MBA program at WCE is newly launched (2026). Placement support via the
   CDC is being established for MBA students, with industry connections being
   built through the existing corporate network.
